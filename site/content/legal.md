## Privacy

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and stores nothing in your browser.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the diagram library (Mermaid) from jsDelivr. These CDNs receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

The media page loads my most recent scrobble through api.ryancranie.com, and its album image is loaded from Last.fm's image CDN, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Some blog posts embed YouTube videos. When one of those embeds loads, YouTube may set its own cookies and collect data under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.
